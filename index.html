A minute to make it yours.

1. Choose a design and update your details.
2. Copy the rendered signature.
3. Paste into Gmail → Settings → Signature, or Apple Mail → Settings → Signatures. In Apple Mail, turn off “Always match my default message font”. Save your changes.

Outlook: paste the rendered version into Settings → Accounts → Signatures. The HTML export uses email tables and system-font fallbacks. Test a message in your own client before using it daily.

Website image URLs are supplied by the brief; confirm they are publicly hosted before installing. The QR is built into the email as HTML cells, so it has no external image dependency. Inbox dark mode here is a simulation; email apps may recolour messages differently.

Social profiles — add confirmed links
Unconfigured profiles are omitted from exported signatures. The website did not provide usable Instagram or Facebook destinations.

Phone number is the brief’s example. Replace it with your real contact number before installing.
